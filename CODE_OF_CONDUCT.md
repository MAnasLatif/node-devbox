# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Node Devbox is an open-source project for people with different backgrounds,
identities, experience levels, and perspectives. Contributors, maintainers,
and users are expected to make participation respectful, constructive, and
free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code, not a person's character or competence.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and receive it in good faith.
- Respect different levels of experience and ask clarifying questions before
  assuming intent.
- Credit other people's work and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Protect private information, credentials, and unpublished security details.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about project scope and moderation.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or demeaning language.
- Sexualized language or attention, including unwelcome advances.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately inflammatory comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Impersonation, plagiarism, or knowingly misleading technical claims.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other spaces where someone is representing the project. It also applies to
private communication about project participation.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an ordinary disagreement, first try to resolve it calmly in the relevant
thread. Report clear or repeated violations to the repository maintainer using
a private contact method listed on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/MAnasLatif). Do not publish
sensitive details in a public issue. If no private contact method is available,
open a minimal issue asking the maintainer to contact you and omit the report's
details.

Maintainers will review reports in context, protect reporters' privacy as far
as reasonably possible, and avoid conflicts of interest. Proportionate action
may include a clarification, warning, content removal, temporary restriction,
or permanent exclusion from project spaces. Knowingly false or bad-faith
reports may also result in action.
